## Root Cause Analysis

### Why Do Our Pinned Patterns Not Match Spalmone?

1. **No LabRotations for G01**: Our LabRotation table has NO entries for G01 groups. The pinned LabBlocks were created by some other mechanism (likely Phase 1 CH phase of the solver).

2. **Solver-Generated Patterns**: The pinned LabBlocks for G01 appear to be generated by the Construction Heuristic phase, not from LabRotation constraints. The solver found a conflict-free placement and we marked them as pinned.

3. **Different Optimization Objective**: The solver optimized for constraint satisfaction, NOT for matching spalmone's patterns. As a result:
   - Informatica moved from Monday (spalmone) to Friday (our solver)
   - Confezione concentrated on Thu+Fri instead of being scattered
   - Tecnologia tessile concentrated on Tuesday instead of scattered

### Impact on Compaction

The solver-generated pinned patterns DO match spalmone in one key aspect: **they use recurring weekly patterns**. However, the SPECIFIC patterns are different:

| Subject | Spalmone Pattern | Our Pattern | Both Recurring? |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Informatica | Monday | Friday | ✅ Yes (but different days) |
| Confezione | Scattered | Thu+Fri | ❌ No |
| Modello | Scattered | Thu+Fri | ✅ Yes (mostly Thu+Fri) |
| Tecnologia tessile | Scattered | Tuesday | ❌ No |

## Hypothesis Validation

**User's hypothesis**: "Our pre-pinning pattern resembles spalmone"

**Finding**: **PARTIALLY TRUE**
- ✅ Both use recurring weekly patterns (same day/time each week)
- ❌ The specific day/time assignments are different
- ❌ Spalmone has more scattered placement (multiple days), we have concentrated placement

## Implications

1. **For G01**: The pinned LabBlocks do NOT constrain compaction in the same way spalmone does. Our patterns are MORE rigid (2 days instead of 5), which should HELP compaction, not hurt it.

2. **For FA groups**: LabRotations exist (G02, G06, G10, G12) and these likely DO match spalmone's recurring patterns.

3. **The compaction problem is NOT caused by pinned LabBlocks for G01** - if anything, our more concentrated patterns should make compaction EASIER than spalmone's scattered approach.

## Recommendation

Investigate WHY the solver achieves worse compaction than spalmone when:
- Our pinned LabBlocks are MORE concentrated (should help compaction)
- We have similar total scheduling units
- The constraints are the same

Possible causes to investigate:
1. Value range filtering (target end dates) may be too restrictive
2. Day-clustered CH phase may not be working correctly
3. Teacher conflict patterns may differ (we track conflicts differently)
