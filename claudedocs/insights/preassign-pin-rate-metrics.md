# Pre-assign pin-rate metrics: lab vs total

> 📌 **Session memory** (operational metric convention).

## Rule

When discussing pre-assignment pin rates, always distinguish between **lab lesson pin rate** and **total lesson pin rate**. They measure different things and should not be compared directly.

## Why

The ~97% figure from earlier sessions was specifically the **lab lesson pin rate** (the primary success metric for pre-assignment). Reporting a 30.1% total pin rate alongside it and implying a regression was misleading — total pin rate includes all non-lab courses, which are never pinned by pre-assignment.

## How to apply

- When reporting pre-assign stats, always show lab pin rate separately from total
- The lab pin rate is the meaningful metric for pre-assignment quality
- Total pin rate is expected to be much lower (~30%) since only lab/rotation courses get pinned
