## Ex-ante prediction (written BEFORE the outcome, 2026-08-05, pre-registered)

The coarse sweep already captured the main effect (~5% medium better at 100 vs 1000). The finer
sweep's job is to locate the sweet spot and check whether lowering below 100 helps or hits a
floor. Prediction:

- **Medium curve: FLAT in 50-200, then gradual degradation toward 1000.** Below the optimum the
  LS under-evaluates moves per step (too greedy) and above it the LS does too few steps; the
  ~5% gain at 100 is most of what there is. So acl=50 is expected to be FLAT with 100 (both near
  the LS-variance floor, ~-1000k medium), not dramatically better. acl=200/500 should land between
  -1000570 (100) and -1051618 (1000), i.e. the curve degrades as acl rises back toward the default.
- **acl=50 feasibility: feasible.** The acl=10000 break was OVER-thorough (forager too exhaustive
  per step, room phases under-converge, -7498 H29). acl=50 is the opposite (under-thorough per
  step, many steps); it should remain feasible (greedy hill-climbing-ish), at most scattering a
  little on medium, not breaking hard.
- **Sweet spot: 50-100.** Most likely the sweep CONFIRMS 100 (or finds 50 marginally better) as
  the optimum, with no gain beyond the ~5% already seen. The practical recommendation would be
  acl=100 (or 50) as an ADOPTION CANDIDATE to lower the production default from 1000, IF the
  ~5% medium gain is robust across the finer points + feasibility holds + no hard/day-start
  regression.

Refutation: if acl=50 under-converges (medium worse than 100, or a hard/day-start regression),
then 100 is the floor and the sweet spot is exactly 100. If the curve is FLAT across 50-500
(everything ~-1000k), then the ACL effect is smaller than the coarse sweep suggested and the
~5% was partly LS variance.

Adoption bar (maintainer's gated decision, post-sweep): a robust ~5% medium gain at the sweet spot,
feasible, no hard/day-start regression, faster wall => candidate to lower the production default
to the sweet spot. The VND-FI staged A/B caveat applies: the acceptor is a WEAK (~5% medium)
lever, so this is a medium-quality tuning, not a hard=0 lever.

## Setup bug: the first finer sweep was INVALIDATED (documented honestly)

The first finer sweep (`blibh1cql`, --no-setup) produced hard around -820 for all of 50/200/500,
incomparable with the prior acl=100/1000 (hard=0). Root cause, confirmed from the captured
lessons.txt (no boot needed): the m32-sweep profile had LOST its pre-assign pins between the
prior acl sweep (2026-08-04) and now. Prior acl-100 had 3090 pinned lessons (true) + 9404
unpinned; the first finer runs had 0 pinned + 12446 unpinned. The `--no-setup` reused a profile
with no pre-assign, so the solver ran on un-pre-assigned data (hard=-820), not the good basin.

**Process lesson: `--no-setup` profile-reuse is FRAGILE. The pre-assign pins (lab rotations +
constrained-teacher, ~3090 lessons) do NOT reliably persist across sessions/days.** This
invalidated a 75-min sweep. Future sweeps MUST use `--setup` (or verify the pinned-lesson count)
to guarantee the pre-assign state. The invalid acl-50/200/500 captures were deleted.

The first finer sweep's only usable signal (even on the wrong state): ACL is FLAT for hard
(~-820 regardless of 50/200/500), consistent with the synthesis (the acceptor does not move
hard). But it says nothing reliable about the medium sweet spot.

## Verdict: NO robust sweet spot. ACL is NOT a reliable adoption candidate.

**Hard: ACL does not move it.** All feasible values (50-1000) keep hard near 0 (the -7/-8/-9 for
the redo is LS/pre-assign-variance level, not an ACL effect); only acl=10000 breaks (-7498 H29,
over-thorough forager, room phases under-converge). This confirms the synthesis (the acceptor is
not a hard lever).

**Medium: the effect is NOT robust at n=1, and the within-state directions CONTRADICT.**
- Prior (3090-pin state): acl=100 (-1000570) is better than 1000 (-1051618). Lower is better.
- Redo (2907-pin state): acl=500 (-1009276) > 200 (-1035978) > 50 (-1037182). Higher is better.

The two clean within-state trends point OPPOSITE ways. The medium-vs-ACL relationship is
STATE-DEPENDENT: its direction depends on the pre-assign state, which itself varies ~6%
run-to-run (the redo's --setup produced 2907 pins vs the prior's 3090; the source=input
pre-assign is NOT fully deterministic). So the ~5% medium range ACL spans is at the
pre-assign-state-variance floor, not a robust, tunable signal.

**acl=50 is fastest (615s, ~2x faster than 100) but lands a worse medium** (-1037182): very
greedy first-improvement converges fast to a worse local optimum. acl=200/500 are slower
(1859/2110s) without beating 100. acl=10000 has the best medium (-945466) but is hard-broken.

## Conclusion (closes TASK-296)

The finer sweep does NOT identify a robust sweet spot. The medium-vs-ACL curve is non-monotonic
and state-dependent at n=1, with contradictory directions across the two clean within-state
comparisons. **ACL is NOT a reliable tuning lever and NOT an adoption candidate.** The only
robust findings: (1) ACL does not move hard; (2) acl=10000 breaks (avoid).

This further refines the synthesis: the "acceptedCountLimit is the one lever that moved the
needle" was an n=1 reading at the LS/pre-assign-variance floor. On closer measurement the
movement is NOT robust (state-dependent, non-monotonic). The acceptor is a WEAK, non-robust
lever, consistent with the synthesis's broader conclusion that on the staged pipeline the
orchestration layer owns the results and the LS metaheuristic is a noise-bound medium knob.

**NO ADOPTION.** The default acl=1000 stands. To pursue ACL further would require n>1 per value
on a FIXED pre-assign state (to separate the ACL effect from the ~6% pre-assign variance), which
is not justified given the synthesis shows the acceptor is a marginal, non-robust lever.

## Side finding (process)

The source=input pre-assign is NOT fully deterministic: ~6% pinned-lesson variance run-to-run
(2907 vs 3090 pins across two --setup runs). This confounds cross-session source=input
comparisons and is the root cause of the first finer sweep's invalidation (the --no-setup reuse
hit a pinless profile). Documented as a process fragility: sweeps must --setup or verify the pin
count, and cross-session source=input comparisons carry this ~6% state noise.
